About

Meet Prabhleen
Arora

I am Prabhleen Arora, a Senior Psychologist and Ph.D. Candidate dedicated to providing a specialized safe space for the LGBTQIA+ community, clients building a healthier relationship with AI and technology, and older adults. Across 3,000+ individual sessions and 40+ workshops reaching 2,500+ participants, my practice is rooted in the belief that therapy must be as unique as the identity of the person seeking it.

My clinical experience spans institutional counseling at Lovely Professional University and ongoing trauma-informed work with Sirat – The Cleft Warriors Ensemble. Combined with my background as a University Topper and Gold Medallist (M.A. Clinical Psychology) and doctoral research into social support systems in LGBT populations, it lets me blend high-level clinical precision with deep social empathy.

My work is specifically designed to address identity affirmation for queer individuals, building a sustainable relationship with AI, and the "Sunday loneliness" often felt by seniors. I bridge the gap between academic research and lived experience to help clients navigate transitions with dignity and resilience.

Whether you are seeking an affirmative space to explore your identity, navigating your relationship with technology, or looking for companionship and clarity in your golden years, I am here, every Sunday.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the practice, the format, and how to begin.

What are Prabhleen Arora's qualifications and experience?

Prabhleen Arora holds an M.A. in Clinical Psychology (University Topper, Gold Medallist) and is a Ph.D. Candidate in Psychology at Lovely Professional University, researching social support systems and cognitive distortions in LGBT populations. She has delivered 3,000+ individual counseling sessions and 40+ workshops reaching 2,500+ participants across institutional and NGO settings, and currently also serves as Senior Psychologist at SynqSol, leading clinical validation of AI-generated psychological reports.

What is queer affirmative therapy?

Queer affirmative therapy is counseling that actively affirms LGBTQIA+ identities rather than treating them as something to be corrected. At Elite Sunday Counseling, it means a non-judgmental space to explore identity, coming out, and relationship dynamics, delivered by a psychologist trained in the specific stresses of the queer experience.

What is AI-affirmative therapy?

AI-affirmative therapy means you won't be judged for using tools like ChatGPT to process your thoughts, journal, or seek quick support between sessions. If that use has become a substitute for human connection or feels hard to control, we work together to build healthier boundaries and a sustainable balance between AI and real-world support.

Why does Elite Sunday Counseling only offer sessions on Sundays?

Sessions are held on Sundays because that's when busy professionals, caregivers, and families finally have uninterrupted time for themselves. No rushing between meetings, no Monday-morning dread. It allows Prabhleen Arora to give each client focused, unhurried attention across global time zones.

How much does a therapy session cost?

A 30-minute Discovery Session costs ₹999 and is designed to understand your needs and confirm fit before any further commitment. A 50-minute Premium Video session is ₹2,999, and a 50-minute Audio Consultation is ₹2,499.

Do you work with international or NRI clients?

Yes. Sessions are held online every Sunday from 11AM to 11PM IST and are open to clients in any global time zone, including the NRI and international diaspora community.

What support is available for the cleft and craniofacial community?

Elite Sunday Counseling supports individuals and families navigating the psychological impact of cleft lip/palate and other craniofacial conditions, including surgical fatigue, social self-consciousness, and building long-term resilience and self-acceptance. Support is also available for parents and caregivers of children with cleft conditions.

What age group is served by the senior wellness counseling?

Senior wellness counseling is aimed at older adults, generally 60 and above, and their caregivers, addressing loneliness, grief, life transitions, and finding renewed purpose.

How do I book my first session?

Fill out the contact form below or email info@elitesundaycounseling.com to book a Discovery Session. You'll hear back within 24 hours to schedule your Sunday appointment.
